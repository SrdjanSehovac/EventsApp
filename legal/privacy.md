## 2. Information we collect

**You give us:**

- **Account:** email address, display name and password (stored only as a one-way hash).
- **Agreement records:** which versions of our Terms, Privacy Policy and Disclaimer you accepted, when, your age confirmation, the app version and the IP address used.
- **RSVPs:** the events you RSVP to, your ticket code, check-in status.
- **Saved events and settings:** events you save, home area, interests and preferences.
- **Content you submit:** event suggestions, posted events, photos, reports about listings, and messages to us.
- **Organizer verification:** business name, website, contact details and proof documents you choose to send.

**Collected automatically:**

- **Location:** if you allow it, your device's approximate or precise location to show events near you. You can turn this off in your phone's settings; you can still search by city.
- **Technical data:** IP address, device type, operating system, app version, and server logs of requests and errors.

**Kept only on your device:** reminders, calendar entries you add to Apple Calendar, and your theme choice. We do not read your calendar.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

## 3. Why we use it

- To create and secure your account and keep a record of the agreements you accepted.
- To show events near you and remember your saved events and settings.
- To process RSVPs and let organizers manage their guest lists and check-in.
- To review, moderate and publish user-submitted events, and to verify organizers.
- To keep the Service safe, prevent fraud and abuse, fix bugs and improve features.
- To send you service messages (for example verification codes or event changes). We will only send marketing messages if you opt in, as required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).
- To meet legal obligations.

## 4. Consent

By creating an account you consent to the uses described here. Location access and notifications are asked for separately by your phone and are optional. You may withdraw consent at any time by changing your phone settings or deleting your account, subject to legal limits. Some features will not work without certain information.

**Young people:** you must be 16 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14; if we learn we have, we will delete it.

## 5. When we share information

- **Organizers:** when you RSVP, the organizer of that event receives your display name, email, RSVP status and check-in status.
- **Service providers** that host and operate the Service for us, such as our cloud server provider (servers located in Canada), email delivery and error monitoring. They may use the information only to provide services to us and must protect it.
- **Legal reasons:** if required by law, court order or a valid request from authorities, or to protect the safety, rights or property of anyone.
- **Business transfer:** if Jaunta is merged, sold or reorganized, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

Some service providers may store or process information outside Quebec or outside Canada (for example in the United States). There, it may be accessible to authorities under local law. For Quebec residents, we assess privacy impacts before transferring information outside Quebec, as Law 25 requires.

If you delete your account, we delete your account, saved events, RSVPs, submitted events and agreement records, normally within 30 days, except limited server logs and copies in encrypted backups, which are overwritten within about 7 days, and anything we must keep by law or to deal with an open dispute. ## 7. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our servers limit access to authorized staff and are backed up. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (and Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information where required).

## 8. Your rights

You may ask to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, withdraw consent, or have it deleted. Quebec residents may also ask for information about how it is used, ask that it be provided in a structured, commonly used technological format (data portability), and ask us to stop disseminating it or de-index it where the law allows. Contact the Privacy Officer; we will reply within 30 days. You may delete your account yourself in **Account & Settings**.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information (cai.gouv.qc.ca).
